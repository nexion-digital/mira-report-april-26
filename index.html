Did email outreach drive website visits? Almost certainly yes. A 90% direct traffic share is abnormal for a startup at this stage. In B2B cold outreach, "direct" almost always means prospects visited the URL directly after receiving an email - GA4 classifies untracked email clicks as direct. Fix: add UTM parameters (e.g. ?utm_source=instantly&utm_medium=email) to every link in Instantly campaigns. This will immediately split outreach traffic from true direct visits in the next report.
